# Descriptions à coller dans Gemini (images)

Déposer les images retenues dans `assets-src/gemini/` avec les noms indiqués. Ne jamais citer de jeu, de film, d'artiste ou de divinité existante dans une description.

## Lot 1 — Références de statues (à passer ensuite dans Meshy « Image vers 3D »)

Voir `docs/MESHY-PROMPTS.md` pour les réglages Meshy. Fichiers attendus dans `assets-src/meshy/` : `colosse.glb`, `sphinx.glb`, `tete.glb`, `brasero.glb`, `idole.glb`.

## Lot 2 — Décor

### Fresque murale — `fresque.png`
```
Generate an image: a long horizontal ancient wall mural painted on sandstone, flat front view, showing a stylized procession of robed figures in profile carrying sun discs toward a large radiant sun, with rows of invented geometric glyphs above and below, faded mineral pigments in turquoise, ochre, deep red and gold leaf, chipped plaster, fine cracks, even flat lighting, no perspective, no frame, no border, no text, no people in modern clothes. Wide panoramic format 4:1, the left and right edges should match so the image can repeat seamlessly.
```

### Frise gravée — `frise.png`
```
Generate an image: a horizontal carved stone frieze seen perfectly from the front, shallow bas-relief of repeating sun discs, lotus-like flowers, wave lines and invented geometric glyphs, pale golden sandstone, soft raking light from the upper left to reveal the relief, weathered edges, no color paint, no text, no frame. Wide format 4:1, seamless repeat on left and right edges.
```

### Motifs de bannières — `banniere-violette.png`, `banniere-rouge.png`, `banniere-turquoise.png`
```
Generate an image: a tall vertical ceremonial cloth banner seen flat from the front, deep violet woven fabric with a large embroidered golden sun emblem in the center, thin gold geometric border, small tassels along the bottom edge, visible fabric weave, slightly faded and frayed, even flat lighting, plain white background, no folds, no pole, no text. Portrait format 1:3.
```
Pour les deux autres, remplacer `deep violet` par `deep crimson red` puis par `turquoise blue`, et `golden sun emblem` par `golden eye-shaped emblem` puis `golden winged disc emblem`.

### Panorama de la cité lointaine — `panorama.png`
```
Generate an image: a very wide matte painting of an immense ancient golden desert city seen from far away at golden hour, layered silhouettes of stepped pyramids, colossal seated statues, obelisks, domes and long colonnades fading into warm atmospheric haze, distant mountains behind, low sun on the left, soft blue sky above with a few thin clouds, no foreground, no people, no text. Ultra wide panoramic format 4:1, horizon at the lower third.
```

### Le Dévoreur — `devoreur.png`
```
Generate an image: a colossal ominous entity made of dark swirling smoke and drifting ash rising behind a distant desert horizon, vaguely humanoid shoulders and a faceless head, two piercing turquoise glowing eyes and a faint glowing fissure where a mouth would be, long tendrils of shadow reaching forward, backlit by a golden sunset haze, seen from very far away, no ground detail, no buildings, no people, no text. Centered subject on a plain pale golden background, portrait format 3:4.
```
